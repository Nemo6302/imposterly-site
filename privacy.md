# Imposterly — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 21 September 2026_

Imposterly has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs, and the game
itself works entirely offline. The only thing that ever leaves your phone is a feedback note, and
only if you choose to send one — see *Feedback you send us* below.

## What stays on your phone

To run the game, Imposterly saves a few things locally on your device only:

- player names and emoji you type in,
- your game settings and selected word packs,
- custom word packs you create,
- a short history of recently dealt words (so rounds don't repeat).

This data never leaves the device, is not backed up by us, and is deleted when you delete the app.
(Your own iCloud device backup may include it, under Apple's iCloud terms.)

## Feedback you send us

*Settings → Send feedback* is the only part of the app that uses the internet, and only at the
moment you tap Send. It is completely optional; the game is identical if you never use it.

**What we receive.** The note you typed (up to 500 characters), whether you tapped *Bug* or *Idea*,
your Imposterly version and your iOS version. Nothing else: no name, no email, no account, no
device, advertising or install identifier — nothing that tells us which phone a note came from, or
that lets us connect two notes to the same person.

**Why we collect it.** To fix the bug or consider the idea. Your note becomes a ticket on our
internal work board and is used for nothing else. We do not use it for advertising or marketing, we
do not profile you, we do not track you, and we never sell it or share it with anyone else.

**Please don't type personal details into the note.** We don't ask for any and we don't need any.
Anything you choose to write about yourself — your name, your email — is stored with the note.

**Where it goes.** The note is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to our database, which is
run by Supabase on Amazon Web Services in Frankfurt, Germany — so it leaves Kenya when you send it.
Supabase and AWS host it on our instructions only, under a jurisdiction with data protection law
comparable to Kenya's. The key inside the app can only add a note; it cannot read any note back.
Only we can read the notes.

**How long we keep it.** Until the ticket is closed, and for up to 12 months after that, then it is
deleted.

**Your rights.** Under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019 you can ask us what we hold about you, ask
for a copy, ask us to correct it, object to our using it, or ask us to delete it. Write to
**pesasahihi@gmail.com** and we will answer within 30 days. One honest limitation: because nothing
in a note identifies you, we cannot look up "your" feedback. To have a note corrected or deleted,
tell us roughly what it said — quote the wording — and about when you sent it, so we can find that
row. If we can't match it, we can't act on it. You can also complain to Kenya's Office of the Data
Protection Commissioner (odpc.go.ke).

**Who is responsible.** Imposterly is published by Aman Shah, Nairobi, Kenya, who is the data
controller for feedback notes. Contact: pesasahihi@gmail.com.

## Children

Imposterly is a general-audience party game and every built-in word pack is family-safe. We do not
knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If a child has sent us a
feedback note, tell us what it said and we will delete it.
